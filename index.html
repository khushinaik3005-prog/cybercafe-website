What is Cyber Security?

Cyber security means protecting computers, mobile phones, networks and personal information from online threats.

📞

Spam Calls

Unwanted calls that may try to steal your personal or financial information.

💬

Spam Messages

Suspicious messages containing fake offers, links or requests for personal information.

🎣

Phishing

Fake emails or websites designed to steal passwords and sensitive information.

Common Cyber Threats

📞 Spam Calls

Spam calls are unwanted calls from unknown people or organizations.

Examples:

  • Fake bank calls
  • Fake KYC calls
  • Lottery calls
  • Fake job offers
Never share your OTP or PIN.

💬 Spam Messages

Spam messages may contain suspicious links or fake offers.

Warning signs:

  • Unknown sender
  • Too-good-to-be-true offers
  • Urgent messages
  • Unknown links
Do not click suspicious links.

🎣 Phishing

Phishing is an attempt to trick people into giving away sensitive information.

Common targets:

  • Passwords
  • Bank information
  • OTP
  • Personal information
Always check the website address.

🔐 Cyber Safety Tips

🔑 Use strong passwords
📱 Keep your apps updated
🚫 Don't share OTP
🔗 Avoid unknown links
🔒 Enable 2-factor authentication
💳 Never share your PIN
📧 Check suspicious emails
🛡️ Use trusted websites
